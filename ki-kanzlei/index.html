Non-negotiable

The three guardrails

§

Confidentiality & data protection

No client data in free consumer tools. The path: a business plan with a data-processing agreement (DPA), training on your data switched off, and anonymize when in doubt. Professional secrecy (§ 203 StGB) and GDPR still apply.

✓

Control before trust

AI occasionally invents things — especially section numbers, case references and figures. Verify every tax-law statement. The responsibility stays with you, always.

→

Start small

Practice on low-stakes tasks first — drafts, summaries, research starting points. Not on the finished tax return. Trust grows with experience.

How to begin well

The method — three moves

A safe space first

A business-grade plan where your data stays yours — data-processing agreement in place, model training switched off. The frame before the first prompt.

Pick one real task

Not "try something out," but something concrete: the next longer client email, or that one research question already sitting on your desk.

Put them side by side

The AI draft next to your own solution. Where does it save time, where not? That’s how you get a feel — honestly and without risk.
