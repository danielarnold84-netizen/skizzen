Important

Three safety rules

§

Protect client data

Never put client data into free tools. Use a business plan with a data contract (AVV), turn off training, and remove names when unsure. Your duty of secrecy (§ 203) and the GDPR still apply.

✓

Check before you trust

AI sometimes invents things — most often numbers, dates and § references. Check every tax statement. The responsibility stays with you.

→

Start small

Begin with easy tasks: drafts, summaries, research. Not with a finished tax return. Trust grows with practice.

What is safe

Safe today — or not yet

🟢 Safe today

  • General research and questions
  • Make internal texts simpler
  • Letter templates (add names by hand later)
  • Summaries of public documents
  • English practice, collecting ideas

🔴 Not yet

  • Real client names
  • Tax numbers and figures
  • Client files and receipts

🟡 Later (with data contract)

  • Anonymous cases: Client A, B, C
  • The AI never sees the real name list

Simple rule: if no client data is in it, you are safe.
